ARCHIVED DEMONSTRATION OUTPUTS

These files were generated by the project's unvalidated heuristic equations
before normal execution was changed to require an explicitly configured
simulator. They are retained only for traceability.

They are not peer-reviewed model results, validated BWR results, BWRX-300
predictions, validated performance improvements, safety analyses, or operating
guidance. Do not cite or present their numeric values as reactor results.

New heuristic-only runs require STARTUP_ESTIMATE_MODE=illustrative and are
written separately under results/illustrative_heuristic/.
